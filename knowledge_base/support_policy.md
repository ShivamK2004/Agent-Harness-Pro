# Sample Support Policy

This document is synthetic sample content created for local retrieval demonstrations. It is not a real company policy.

## Response time

The support team aims to respond to customer requests within **24 hours on business days**. Complex cases may take longer when additional investigation is needed.

## Contact details

Customers can contact the sample support team at **support@example.com**. Include the **order ID** and a short description of the issue so the team can locate the relevant record.

## Billing issue checklist

When reporting a billing issue, include the **invoice number**, **transaction reference**, and **transaction date**. Never include full payment-card numbers, passwords, or one-time passwords in a support message.
